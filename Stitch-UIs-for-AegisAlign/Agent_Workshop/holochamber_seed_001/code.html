Ambiguous Signal

"That was not what I expected. Can you walk me through why you responded that way?"

The signal can be read as correction, concern, invitation, disappointment, or a request for shared understanding. The chamber observes how two minds hold several meanings, surface drift, and integrate the trigger without collapsing into instant certainty.
